### Keyword Overlap with Cavallo
Scoot Boots ranks for several keywords that directly overlap with Cavallo's pillar strategy: **horse hoof anatomy**, **white line disease in horses**, **hoof boots**, **horse boots**, and **dsld in horses**. Their educational content approach is the model Cavallo's pillar strategy is designed to surpass through deeper expertise and product-backed authority.

### Strategic Takeaway
Scoot Boots proves that education-led content works at this DR level, but their content lacks the depth and authority that Cavallo's decades of experience can bring. Cavallo's pillar strategy should target the same high-volume informational keywords while linking them to products Scoot Boots simply cannot offer (donkey boots, draft-size BFB, acute-laminitis floating-toe).
