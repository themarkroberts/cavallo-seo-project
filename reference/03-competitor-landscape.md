## Key Insights

### Domain Authority
- **EasyCare** has the highest DR (54), but underperforms relative to that authority
- **Cavallo** and **Scoot Boots** are tied at DR 48 — Scoot Boots converts this into nearly 2x the traffic through educational content
- **Renegade** (DR 38) and **Softride** (DR 32) have lower authority but strong niche positions

### Traffic Trends
- All five competitors show seasonal patterns, with summer peaks and winter troughs
- Cavallo has seen the steepest decline (-46% from peak), suggesting lost ground on informational queries
- Scoot Boots maintains the most consistent traffic through their blog content strategy

### Keyword Landscape
- **Scoot Boots** leads in total keywords (492) and targets high-volume informational terms that overlap directly with Cavallo's three pillars
- **EasyCare** has many keywords (464) but poor positions on high-volume terms — an opportunity for Cavallo to leapfrog them
- **Renegade** dominates product-intent keywords ("hoof boots" #1, "best horse boots for trail riding" #1) despite low DR
- **Softride** validates demand for founder/navicular/ringbone content (Pillar 2)

## Strategic Takeaway

Cavallo has a structural advantage that no competitor can match: decades of hoof care expertise, the broadest product line in the industry (including exclusive products like donkey boots, draft-size BFB, and acute-laminitis floating-toe), and a DR on par with the market leader. The pillar content strategy is designed to convert this authority and experience into search visibility by targeting the same informational keywords competitors rank for — then routing that traffic to products only Cavallo offers.
