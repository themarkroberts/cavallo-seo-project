### Keyword Overlap with Cavallo
EasyCare ranks for many founder/laminitis-related keywords that align with Cavallo's Pillar 2 (Hoof Health and Conditions): **foundered horse**, **horse founder**, **foundering horse**. ### Strategic Takeaway
EasyCare's higher DR (54 vs Cavallo's 48) gives them a backlink advantage, but their keyword rankings are surprisingly poor for their authority level. Many high-volume keywords sit at positions 15-50+. Cavallo can leapfrog EasyCare on founder/laminitis content by publishing deeper, more authoritative pillar content backed by product solutions.
